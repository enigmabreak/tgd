TGDownloader Documentation

Welcome to TGDownloader, a high-speed, multi-account autonomous download engine built specifically for downloading large educational courses, videos, and multi-gigabyte queues from any Telegram channel without bandwidth throttling or account restrictions.

Architectural Features

1. Multi-Account Fleet Pooling & Load Balancing

Telegram restricts standard free accounts to ~20–30 Mbps (~2.5–3.5 MB/s) of download throughput. TGDownloader breaks through this protocol ceiling by managing an intelligent pooling architecture across independent account namespaces (default, acc2, acc3, etc.):

  • Isolated Session Spaces: Each added account maintains its own isolated crypto session and connection states.
  • Automatic Dynamic Load Balancing: Clicking Load Distribution in the Tasks Queue balances topics evenly across all accounts in your fleet so downloads execute in parallel at maximum aggregate throughput.
  • Smart Fallback: If an account encounters a Telegram flood limit or temporary DC rate cap, other workers continue running without interrupting queue progress.

2. Zero-Friction QR Pairing & Profile Detection

Adding an account requires no technical configuration, API credentials, phone numbers, or SMS verification codes:

  • Open the Account tab and click + New Account.
  • Open Telegram on your smartphone (→ Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device) and scan the QR code.
  • The engine automatically inspects the session token, detects your User ID and Username (@handle), and registers the account into your fleet immediately.

3. High-Throughput Stream Scaling & Connection Pooling

Because each free Telegram account delivers approximately 25 Mbps (~3.1 MB/s) of steady throughput, aggregate fleet throughput scales linearly with your internet connection:

Internet Connection Recommended Fleet Expected Throughput
50 Mbps 2 Free Accounts ~6.2 MB/s aggregate
100 Mbps 4 Free Accounts ~12.5 MB/s aggregate
200 Mbps 8 Free Accounts ~25.0 MB/s aggregate
300+ Mbps 12+ Free Accounts / Premium Full line saturation

Each active topic stream operates with 8 parallel chunk threads and a 16-connection DC pool for optimal packet delivery.

4. Dynamic Topic Configuration & Post Ranges

You can add topics from any public or joined Telegram channel by entering its link (e.g. https://t.me/channel_name) or @username. The download engine supports flexible post syntax:

  • Single Post ID: 1722
  • Comma-Separated List: 1722, 1725, 1730
  • Continuous Range: 1743..1788 (all posts between 1743 and 1788 inclusive)
  • Mixed Range Syntax: 1722..1731, 1948..1952, 1996..2008

Expand Advanced Options to filter by media type (Videos Only, Documents Only, or Custom Extensions) and fine-tune thread/pool concurrency per topic.

5. Storage Target Recommendation & Cloud Drives

When creating a topic, TGDownloader automatically inspects your physical disks and highlights the drive with the highest free space (e.g. ★ Recommended: Drive E:\). You can also download straight into Google Drive for Desktop (mounted as G:\My Drive) or OneDrive, streaming files directly into cloud storage without consuming local drive space.

Automation & Performance

6. Automated Telegram Mobile Push Alerts

Receive instant notifications on your phone whenever a topic finishes downloading, if a network error occurs, or when the full queue completes:

  • TGDownloader automatically links to your paired Telegram User ID.
  • Customizable completion and auto-shutdown alerts allow personalized messages to be delivered straight to your Telegram app.
  • No manual bot configuration required, operating autonomously via one-click activation.

7. Windows Defender 1-Click Optimization

During multi-chunk high-speed downloads, Windows Defender's Antimalware Service (MsMpEng.exe) scans incoming chunks as they hit the disk, causing CPU spikes and disk throttling. Clicking Add Windows Defender Exclusion in the Power Watchdog tab registers the directory with Windows Security, immediately freeing CPU cycles and enabling full NVMe/SSD speeds.

8. Overnight Power Watchdog

Built for set-and-forget overnight operation:

  • Prevent PC Sleep: Keeps Windows awake while download workers are actively streaming.
  • Auto-Shutdown PC on Completion: Automatically powers down your PC cleanly after all queued downloads complete.

9. Native Terminal & Instant Stop

Monitor transfer progress, chunk rates, and status logs in real time in the Terminal & Logs tab, powered by low-latency native Electron IPC. Clicking Stop terminates all running background download processes cleanly in under 0.1 seconds.
